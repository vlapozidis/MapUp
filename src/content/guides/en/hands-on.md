🚀 This is where all the theory turns into muscle memory. You're getting your own sandbox — go ahead and click around, break things, and fix them. That's what it's for.

## Why hands-on matters

You can read about backups and HADR all day, but nothing teaches it like actually restoring a database when you're a little tired and the steps don't *quite* match what's on screen. This is a safe place to have that experience *before* it happens for real.

## 🎁 What you'll get

- **A demo Azure subscription** — your own space for labs, databases, VMs, and DBA tasks. Nothing you do here affects production, ever.
- **Help installing essential tools** on your PC:
  - **VMware / virtualization tools** — for running local test environments
  - **SQL Server** — the engine you'll be administering
  - **SSMS (SQL Server Management Studio)** — your main day-to-day tool. Make sure you're on [version 20](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/sql/ssms/release-notes-ssms) or later; it's required.
  - **Additional tools as needed** — your team will point you to anything role-specific

## 🏷️ Skills you'll pick up

`Virtual Machines` `SQL Server` `SSMS` `Networking Basics` `Sandbox Environments`

## 📩 What happens next

Once your subscription is activated, you'll get detailed connection instructions along with your **first lab tasks**. These are designed to mirror real DBA work — restoring a backup, tuning a slow query, setting up a maintenance job — just in a space where nothing bad can happen if you get it wrong.

## 💡 Tips

- Screenshot or write down anything confusing as you go — it makes for great questions in your next 1:1.
- It's completely normal for the first few labs to take longer than you expect. That's not a sign you're behind; it's just what learning a new environment looks like.
- Once you're comfortable here, ask about shadowing a first support rotation — that's where things get really real (in a good way). 🎉
