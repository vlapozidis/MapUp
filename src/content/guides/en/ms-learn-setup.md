👋 Welcome aboard! Your very first onboarding task is a quick one: getting set up on **Microsoft Learn**, the platform that hosts all of your official training.

## 🔑 Use a personal account — not your company one

Your DBA training runs through Microsoft Learn using a **personal Microsoft account**, not your work account. This keeps your learning history and badges tied to *you*, no matter where your career takes you next.

## 📝 How to sign in

1. Go to [learn.microsoft.com](https://learn.microsoft.com/).
2. Click **Sign in** (top right).
3. Select **Create one!** if you don't already have an account.
4. Use a personal email address (Outlook, Gmail, Yahoo, etc.) — whatever you already check daily.
5. Once you're logged in, you can track your progress, collect badges, and jump into learning paths any time.

## 🎯 What you're actually working toward

> Our goal is for you to complete the training **modules** — not necessarily the certification exams. Learning the material is what matters most right now.

## ✅ Quick checklist

- [ ] Personal Microsoft account created
- [ ] Signed in at learn.microsoft.com
- [ ] Bookmarked the site (you'll be here a lot!)

## 💡 Tips for new hires

- Microsoft Learn autosaves your progress, so it's totally fine to do a module in 15-minute chunks between meetings.
- Collecting badges is genuinely satisfying — don't be afraid to enjoy it. 🏅
- Stuck signing in? Ping your onboarding buddy before you spend more than 5 minutes fighting with it.
