📚 Microsoft Learn is your primary path, but sometimes a different teaching style just clicks better — that's what these platforms are for.

## Why bother with extra material?

Not every concept lands the first time, and that's fine. If a Microsoft Learn module leaves you with questions, searching for the same topic on one of these platforms can give you a completely different — and sometimes clearer — explanation.

## 🎥 Pluralsight

[app.pluralsight.com](https://app.pluralsight.com/id)

Great for structured, expert-led courses. Search for topics like "Azure SQL fundamentals" or "T-SQL for beginners" if you want a second pass at something from DP-900 or DP-300.

## 🎓 Udemy

[udemy.com](https://www.udemy.com/organization/global-login/email)

Good for hands-on, project-style courses — useful once you've got the fundamentals down and want to build something concrete.

## 🛠️ Operations videos

[Operations Archive](https://vertitech365.sharepoint.com/sites/OperationsArchive/SitePages/CollabHome.aspx)

Internal recordings covering real processes and past incidents — genuinely useful once you've got some fundamentals under your belt.

> 🔐 Login credentials for Pluralsight and Udemy are shared separately by your onboarding contact — they aren't published here for security reasons.

## 💡 Tips

- Treat this as a *supplement*, not a checklist to complete. Nobody expects you to watch every course on these platforms.
- If you find a course that explained something really well, share it in your team channel — future new hires will thank you. 🙌
