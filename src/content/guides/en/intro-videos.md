🎥 Alongside the Microsoft Learn paths, take some time to watch our internal introduction videos — they're short, and they'll save you weeks of "wait, who do I ask about that?"

## 👋 Why watch these

Microsoft Learn teaches you Azure and SQL. It doesn't teach you *how our team actually works* — the tools we favor, the way we communicate, and who owns what. That's exactly what these videos cover.

## 📺 Where to find them

👉 [onboarding.classter.com](https://onboarding.classter.com/)

We'd suggest watching one or two per day rather than binging them all at once — there's a lot to absorb in week one already.

## 🧑‍💼 Questions? Reach out

You don't have to figure any of this out alone. If something in the videos doesn't make sense, or you just want a friendly face to ask a "silly question" to (there's no such thing, by the way), reach out to:

## 💡 A tip from people who've done this before

Keep a running list of questions as you go through onboarding — little things you're curious about but that don't block you right now. Bring the list to your first 1:1. It's a great way to make the most of that time.
